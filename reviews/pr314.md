## Additional findings

Adds 5 EFI variable attribute constants and corresponding print statements that were missing
from the lsefivar command: HARDWARE_ERROR_RECORD (0x08), AUTHENTICATED_WRITE_ACCESS (0x10),
TIME_BASED_AUTHENTICATED_WRITE_ACCESS (0x20), APPEND_WRITE (0x40), and
ENHANCED_AUTHENTICATED_ACCESS (0x80). All are defined in the UEFI specification but were
previously absent from GRUB's headers and lsefivar output.
