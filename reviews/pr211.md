## Additional findings

The three LVM commits (3-5 by topic order) are the bulk of the
change and form a cohesive unit: `grub_lvm_free_lv()` /
`grub_lvm_free_vg()` helpers centralize cleanup, node names are
now `grub_strdup()`'d in the ignored-feature deep copy (preventing
double-free), and `ignored_feature_lvs` is moved to function scope
so every `goto fail4` releases it. The `grub_zalloc` for the VG
ensures the helper can walk NULL pointer lists safely on early
error paths. The GPT fix correctly uses `grub_uint64_t` for
`last_offset` and a `while` carry loop instead of the previous
exact-match `if` that failed for entry sizes not dividing 512.
