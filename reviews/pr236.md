## Additional findings

The claim checks out: `dilithium.c` `#include`s `dilithium-common.c` (line 517) and
`dilithium-dep.c` (lines 613/696/772/848), and neither is a separate compilation unit — only
`dilithium.c` is listed (`common =`) in the `pubkey` module. Files that are only `#include`d
are not in any `_SOURCES`, so automake omits them from `make dist`, and an extracted-tarball
build fails exactly as the commit message shows. `extra_dist` is the right mechanism and is
added to the correct module, next to `common = ...dilithium.c`.

The fix is complete for the core-module tarball: `Makefile.core.def` is hand-maintained (the
`dilithium.c` line was added with the ML-DSA appendedsig work in `c59c042bf`), while the
other `#include`-only cipher helpers (`camellia.c`, `hash-common.c`) belong to auto-generated
modules emitted by `util/import_gcry.py` into `Makefile.gcry.def`, which handles their
distribution. Dilithium is the hand-integrated exception, so it is the only one that needed
this.
