# AI Review: MR !301 - lib/datetime: Fix NO_CAST.INTEGER_OVERFLOW

1 commit fixes a Coverity `NO_CAST.INTEGER_OVERFLOW` finding in `grub_unixtime2datetime()`:
`days_epoch * SECPERDAY` was computed in 32-bit `int` arithmetic (both operands are `int`)
before being subtracted from the 64-bit `nix`, which overflows once `days_epoch` exceeds
`INT_MAX / SECPERDAY` (~24855 days, i.e. dates from 2038 onward).

**Commits:**
1. **f59fc882c** - Fix: NO_CAST.INTEGER_OVERFLOW

## Issues Found

No issues found.

## Additional findings

Casting `days_epoch` to `grub_int64_t` before the multiplication promotes the whole
expression to 64-bit, matching `nix`'s type, so the subtraction into `secs_in_day` is
computed correctly for both the pre-1970 (`nix < 0`) and post-1970 branches. The fix is
scoped to exactly the flagged line; `days_epoch` itself remains a 32-bit `int` (unchanged by
this commit, pre-existing), but that is a separate, much larger-range concern not touched
here.
