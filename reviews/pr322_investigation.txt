== Method ==

  rtb cat grub-core/commands/efi/lsefivar.c --ref origin/master  > old.c
  rtb cat grub-core/commands/efi/lsefivar.c --ref pr322          > new.c

internal whitespace differences are discounted -- i.e. no token, operator, string literal,

stronger guarantee than eyeballing the unified diff, which would require manually re-deriving the
same conclusion hunk by hunk across roughly 140 changed lines.
