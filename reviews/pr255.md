## Additional findings

"Enumerate once" (commit 12) is a documented design trade-off: after the first iteration the `valid` flags are frozen
(obdisk.c ~1167-1175), so a hot-unplugged device stays listed and a hot-added one is not listed. A stale entry costs one
listed name plus a `Can't open device` line (`ensure_open()`, ~697-701); `grub_disk_open` tries the next driver and
`iterate_disk` clears `grub_errno`, so nothing hangs, crashes or opens another device. By-name open still works, and a
failed by-name lookup of an `ieee1275/` name re-enumerates (`find_grub_devpath()`, ~1219-1241) and heals the list.
Enumerating on `GRUB_DISK_PULL_RESCAN` would bring the cost back, as the commit message says.

For more details, see
[pr255_investigation.txt](https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/pvalena/grub/-/blob/main/reviews/pr255_investigation.txt).
