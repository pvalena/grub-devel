## Additional findings

The clamp mirrors the existing leaf-array clamp in
`grub_ext2_read_block()` (added by 7e2f750f0a). Since
`grub_ext4_extent_header`, `grub_ext4_extent`, and
`grub_ext4_extent_idx` are all 12 bytes, the `-1` accounting (one
header slot) produces the same capacities: 4 inline entries
(`sizeof(inode->blocks) / 12 - 1`), and 84 entries at the minimum
1024-byte block size (`1024 / 12 - 1`). The `buf == NULL` test to
distinguish inline from external is correct: `buf` is NULL on the
first iteration (inline inode blocks) and allocated on all
subsequent iterations (external extent blocks).
