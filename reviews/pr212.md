## Additional findings

The `grub_mm_header.size` field counts cells including the header
cell itself (documented in `mm_private.h`). The old
`grub_memcpy(q, ptr, p->size << GRUB_MM_ALIGN_LOG2)` copied one
header-cell worth of bytes beyond the old block's payload. The
fix to `(p->size - 1)` is correct and safe: the new zero-size
guard ensures the subtraction cannot underflow, and a one-cell
block (from `grub_malloc(0)`) copies zero bytes.
