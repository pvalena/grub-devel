## Issues Found

### Issue 1: signed_attrs.raw leaked in pkcs7_free_signers

**Location:** `grub-core/commands/appendedsig/pkcs7.c`,
`pkcs7_free_signers()`, line ~812

`signed_attrs.raw` is allocated with `grub_malloc` in
`pkcs7_get_signerinfo_signed_attrs()` (line ~330) and stored in
the signer struct. `pkcs7_free_signers()` zeroes the
`signed_attrs` struct with `grub_memset` but does not
`grub_free(signers->signed_attrs.raw)` first. The pointer is
lost and the buffer leaks for each signer with signedAttrs.

Compare with `signers->serial` and `signers->issuer` on the
lines immediately above, which are properly freed before the
memset.

**Draft fix:**

```diff
--- a/grub-core/commands/appendedsig/pkcs7.c
+++ b/grub-core/commands/appendedsig/pkcs7.c
       grub_free (signers->serial);
       grub_free (signers->issuer);
       grub_memset (&signers->md_algo, 0x00, sizeof (grub_pkcs7_mdalgo_t));
+      grub_free (signers->signed_attrs.raw);
       grub_memset (&signers->signed_attrs, 0x00, sizeof (grub_pkcs7_signedattr_t));
```
