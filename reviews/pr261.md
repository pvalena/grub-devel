## Additional findings

`key_size` is read by `protect_tpm2_add()` (`key_size > TPM_MAX_SYM_DATA`) after
`protect_read_file(args->tpm2_keyfile, (void **)&key, &key_size)`. `key_size` is genuinely
set on the success path before that read; the compiler's warning under `-O2` is a false
positive from the inlined `protect_tpm2_seal()` call chain (`protect_tpm2_add` ->
`protect_tpm2_seal`, where `key_size` becomes `clearTextLength` and feeds a `memcpy` size),
not a real uninitialized-use bug. Initializing `key_size = 0` at declaration is the standard,
behavior-preserving way to silence this class of warning without restructuring control flow.
The change is a single-line declaration edit with no functional effect on any reachable path.
