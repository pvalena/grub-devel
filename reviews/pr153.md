## Additional findings

The conditional `if (grub_dl_ref_count(mod) == 1) grub_dl_unref(mod)`
correctly handles both paths: autoloaded modules at ref_count 0 skip
the unref (avoiding underflow), while `insmod`-loaded modules at
ref_count 1 get the unref before `grub_dl_unload`. The `> 1` refusal
threshold is unchanged, preserving the CVE-2020-25632 boundary.

The regression test (160 lines) covers three paths: ref_count==0
rmmod (gzio), insmod/rmmod round-trip (hexdump), and unknown module
error. Each test has lsmod preconditions to prevent vacuous passes.
