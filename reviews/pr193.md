## Additional findings

`protocols_per_handle` allocates via UEFI `AllocatePool`; the buffer
was never freed, leaking each iteration of the handle loop. The fix
calls `grub_efi_free_pool(protocols)` after the protocol enumeration
loop. `grub_efi_allocate_pool` is added for completeness but unused
in this commit.
